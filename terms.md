## 2. Purpose & Scope

Vagudle Bot provides commands and features for playing Vagudle, a word-guessing game, through Discord. This includes:

* Generating and sharing challenge links (`/vagudle_challenge`)
* Starting and tracking duels against other users, including via Discord Activities (`/vagudle_duel`)
* Configuring and posting the shared daily word for a server or group (`/vagudle_daily_channel`)
* Viewing duel and daily leaderboards (`/vagudle_duel_leaderboard`, `/vagudle_daily_leaderboard`)
* Delivering Direct Message notifications for duel results and duel expiry
